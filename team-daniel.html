What they've done

  • Economic research intern at the Central Bank of Indonesia — researched Overnight Indexed Swaps across domestic and global markets, assessing their implications for Indonesia's financial stability, and built reports and presentations on monetary policy and asset securities data for internal policy briefings.

  • External Affairs, PERMIAS Purdue (Indonesian Student Association) — coordinates with PERMIAS chapters nationwide and serves as liaison with the Indonesian Embassy in Chicago on national events and planning.
  • Public Relations, Sampoerna Young Investor Club (Jakarta) — led "Sekolah Pasar Modal," a collaborative event with the Indonesia Stock Exchange, Mirae Asset Securities and Sampoerna University on investment trends and market forecasting.
  • Power BI, Python, Java, R, Excel and Looker Studio; Microsoft Office Specialist (Excel), McKinsey Forward, and BCG Strategy Consulting simulation. Bilingual in English and Bahasa Indonesia.
