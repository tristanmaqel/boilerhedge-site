The curriculum

Every session pairs a lecture with an interactive activity, workshop, or speaker. From "what is a hedge fund" to building your own backtest — no prerequisites assumed.

Semester One
Week 1

Hedge Funds — Industry & Careers

  • Kickoff social
  • Hedge fund overview, strategies & alternative investments
  • Hedge funds vs. traditional asset managers
  • Roles & institutional structure; buy-side vs. sell-side
  • Discussion: "Which role fits you?"
Week 3

Fundamental Investing

  • Valuation basics: DCF, WACC, CAPM
  • Catalysts & shorting mechanics
  • Team stock pitch mini-competition
Week 5

Quant Strategies I — Factor Investing

  • Factor models: momentum, size, quality
  • The Fama-French three-factor model
  • Simple portfolio construction from factor baskets
Week 7

Quant Strategies II — Market Neutral

  • Statistical arbitrage overview
  • Beta-neutral & sector-neutral portfolios; pairs trading
  • Portfolio construction in multi-PM contexts
  • Python workshop: build a simple backtest
Week 9

Risk & Performance Metrics

  • Sharpe, Sortino, Value-at-Risk, and more
  • Python workshop: computing risk metrics
Week 11

Market Microstructure & Trading Simulations

  • Market structure: orders, liquidity, execution
  • Order flow & transaction mechanics
  • Trading simulations & introductory algorithms
Week 13

Derivatives & Hedging

  • Futures, options, and swaps
  • Leverage & risk management
  • Case study: the SNB and margin calls
  • Mini simulation: hedge a portfolio with derivatives
Semester Two
Session 1

Guest Speakers — Hedge Fund & AM Professionals

  • Careers and recruiting
  • Strategy & alternative investments discussion
  • Open Q&A
Session 2

Quant Strategies III — Machine Learning

  • ML models in markets: overfitting, feature selection, challenges
  • Team data-modeling challenge
Session 3

Event-Driven & Special Situations

  • Merger arbitrage
  • Restructurings & activism; earnings plays
  • Case study discussions
Session 4

Global Macro & Fixed Income

  • How macro funds invest
  • Rates, FX, and commodities
  • Macro data and market reactions
Session 5

Risk Management & Portfolio Stress Testing

  • Multi-PM risk frameworks; leverage & VaR
  • Portfolio blow-ups: LTCM, Archegos
  • Interactive simulation: portfolio stress test
Finale

Comprehensive Hedge Fund Case Competition

  • Team presentations: long/short, quant, or macro ideas
  • Panel discussion & final strategy showcase
  • Networking

Draft syllabus — topics and order may shift as the semester takes shape.

Learn it, then trade it.

Every topic above comes with a hands-on component. The only requirement is showing up.
