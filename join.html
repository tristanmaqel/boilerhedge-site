Join BoilerHedge

Open to every major and every experience level. No finance knowledge, no coding — just show up curious.

How to get involved

Three steps, none of them hard.

Fill out the interest form

Two minutes. Tells us who you are and gets you on the mailing list.

Open the interest form →

Come to a meeting

We meet every second or third week during the semester. Room, times, and lectures announced each semester.

Dive in

Join a stock pitch, trading simulation, or Python workshop. Members take part in at least one competition or challenge per semester.

What we ask of members

  • No prerequisites. No prior finance or coding knowledge required — the curriculum starts from zero.
  • Any major. There are no academic major requirements.
  • Participate. Take part in at least one competition, simulation, or challenge per semester.
  • Collaborate. Disciplined risk-taking and analytical thinking, learned together — that's the whole point.

Questions first?

Email us at hfspurdue@gmail.com, or reach out to any officer on the team page — we answer.
